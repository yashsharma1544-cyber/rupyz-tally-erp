============================================================

============================================================

voucher-type scoping, reading 8 source tabs from your Google Sheet.

PREREQ: you already ran 56_tally_targets.sql and set the Google env vars.

STEP 3 — LOAD DATA
------------------------------------------------------------
After deploy, open Tally Targets → click "Refresh from Google Sheet".
It reads all 8 tabs, tags company + voucher_type, loads ~24,100 rows.
The response lists per-tab row counts (perTab) so you can confirm each tab read.

Then verify in SQL:
  select * from tally_companies();        -- Sushil + Anjali with totals

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU GET
------------------------------------------------------------
Tally Targets page now has a COMPANY selector (Sushil / Anjali) at the top.
Pick company → beat → enter target → splits to shops by that company's Tally kg.
Targets are stored per (company, beat, period) so the two companies never mix.

NOTE on the refresh: tab titles are matched by PREFIX, so the truncated names
in Google ("SUSHIL AGENCIES 26-27 - GST SAL...") resolve to their full titles
automatically. If a tab gets renamed, update the SOURCES list in route.ts.
